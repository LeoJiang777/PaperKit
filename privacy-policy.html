PaperKit Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

1. Overview

PaperKit is a utility app provided by LIANG JIANG. It helps users compress images, convert images to PDF, convert PDF pages to images, scan QR codes, and generate QR codes. This policy explains how PaperKit handles information and how third-party services used by the app may process data.

2. Information We Process

PaperKit processes selected files locally on your device. We do not upload your selected images or PDF files to our own servers.

  • Images and PDF files selected by you are used only to complete the requested local processing task.
  • QR code scan results, such as text or links, are processed locally on your device to display, copy, or open the result.
  • Text or links entered for QR code generation are processed locally on your device to create the QR code image.
  • Generated files are saved to your device, such as Photos, Downloads, or other system-approved local locations.
  • Generated QR code images are saved to Photos only when you choose to save them.
  • We do not create user accounts or intentionally collect your name, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

3. Permissions

PaperKit may use the following permissions or system capabilities:

  • System file picker: used to let you choose images or PDF files for processing.
  • Camera permission: used only when you choose to scan a QR code. Camera frames are used for scanning on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Storage write permission: used only on Android 9 and below to save generated files.
  • Photos or media storage access: used through Android system-approved storage features to save generated files, including compressed images, PDF output, exported PDF page images, and QR code images.
  • Internet access: used to load ads and privacy consent messages.

4. Ads and Third-Party SDKs

PaperKit uses Google AdMob to display ads and may use the Google User Messaging Platform SDK to present privacy consent messages. Google AdMob may collect or process device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and data related to ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention according to Google's policies.

Learn more from Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Learn how Google uses data from partner apps and sites: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Privacy Choices

Where required, PaperKit uses the Google User Messaging Platform to provide ad privacy choices. You may choose whether to allow personalized ads through the consent prompt. You may also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

6. Data Retention

Because PaperKit does not upload your selected files to our own servers, we do not store your images, PDFs, or generated files on our servers. Data processed by third-party advertising services is handled according to the relevant third party's privacy policy and retention rules.

7. Children's Privacy

PaperKit is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

8. International Data Transfers

Third-party advertising and related services may process data outside your country or region. Such processing is performed by the relevant third party according to its own policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or third-party service changes. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
